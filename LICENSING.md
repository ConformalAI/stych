# Licensing

## Current license

Stych is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 only** (`AGPL-3.0-only`). The full text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

In short: you can run, study, and modify Stych freely. If you distribute a modified version, or let others use a modified version over a network, you must make the source of your modified version available under the AGPL. The AGPL covers the Stych software only, not your data or the outputs Stych produces for you.

If the AGPL does not work for you, ask the maintainers about a commercial license.

## Open core and enterprise features

Stych is built as an open core. Most of the product is open source under the AGPL in this repository. We plan to offer a smaller set of enterprise features separately, in their own directory with their own license. Nothing in this repository is under a commercial license today.

## Future intent

This is a statement of direction, not a binding promise.

- We intend to move the open core to a more permissive license, most likely the **Apache License 2.0**, once the open-core and enterprise split is complete and the project has an active user and customer base.
- The direction is toward more permissive terms, not more restrictive ones. Code you already received under the AGPL stays available to you under the AGPL.
- The [CLA](CLA.md) is what lets us make this change without tracking down every past contributor.
